| Service Area | Focus | Typical Format |
|---|---|---|
| Worship Service | Praise, Scripture, preaching, response | In-person and online gathering |
| Prayer Service | Intercession, thanksgiving, spiritual covering | Corporate prayer meeting |

| Fasting Program | Spiritual discipline, consecration, guidance | Individual and group fasting windows |

| Worship & Prayer Night | Extended worship and intercession | Dedicated evening gathering |
