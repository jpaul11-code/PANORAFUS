# Release Certificate & Degree Registration

A formal registry for participants who have completed courses, programs, and seminaries through the **PANORAFUS.AI Online Schools Program**.

## 📜 About This Registry

This page serves as the official record for **Release Certificates** and **Degrees** awarded to participants who have successfully completed accredited study programs under PANORAFUS.AI. Certificates and degrees affirm the completion of theological, religious, and ministry formation courses offered through PANORAFUS.AI's Online Schools Program and affiliated Seminaries.

---

## 🎓 Types of Awards

| Award Type | Description | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate of Completion | Awarded upon finishing a short course or module | 1–12 weeks |
| Certificate of Ministry | Awarded for completing a foundational ministry training program | 3–6 months |
| Certificate in Theological Studies | Awarded for completing an introductory theology curriculum | 6–12 months |
| Diploma in Biblical Studies | Awarded for completing a structured diploma-level program | 1 year |
| Associate Degree in Ministry | Awarded for completing an associate-level ministry program | 2 years |
| Bachelor of Theology (B.Th.) | Awarded for completing a full undergraduate theology program | 3–4 years |
| Master of Divinity (M.Div.) | Awarded for completing a graduate-level divinity program | 3 years |
| Doctor of Ministry (D.Min.) | Awarded for completing a doctoral-level ministry program | 3+ years |

## 📋 Certificate & Degree Registration Form

Participants who have completed a PANORAFUS.AI program may register their award by submitting the following information to the PANORAFUS.AI administration:

### Required Information

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Full Legal Name** | Participant's full name as it will appear on the certificate |
| **Program Name** | Name of the course, diploma, or degree program completed |
| **Award Type** | Certificate / Diploma / Degree (see types above) |
| **School / Seminary Name** | Name of the PANORAFUS.AI school or affiliated seminary |
| **Date of Completion** | Date the program was officially completed (DD/MM/YYYY) |
| **Program Duration** | Length of the program completed |
| **Instructor / Dean Name** | Name of the lead instructor or academic dean |
| **Contact Email** | Participant's email address for certificate delivery |
| **Mailing Address** | Physical address if a printed certificate is requested |

## 📬 How to Register

1. **Complete your program** — Finish all required coursework and assessments.
2. **Request your certificate** — Email the administration at [paul@seasonedchristianministrychurch.community](mailto:paul@seasonedchristianministrychurch.community) with the subject line: **"Certificate Registration — [Your Full Name]"**.
3. **Provide required information** — Include all fields listed in the Registration Form section above.
4. **Await confirmation** — The administration will verify your completion and issue your certificate or degree within 14–30 business days.
5. **Receive your award** — Certificates are issued digitally (PDF) and, upon request, as a printed document.
