## Overview

PANORAFUS.AI is a ministry-rooted, mission-driven global network. Compensation arrangements — including author honoraria, contributor stipends, institutional licensing fees, and partnership agreements — are handled with full transparency and in alignment with our core values of integrity, service, and Scripture-rooted accountability.

---

## Compensation Structure

| Role / Arrangement | Description |
|---|---|
| 📖 **Author / Content Contributor** | Honoraria may be offered for original theological studies, devotional content, and institutional profiles contributed to the network |
| 🎓 **Curriculum & Educational Content** | Compensation for accredited course materials submitted to PANORAFUS.AI Online Schools Program |
| 🤝 **Institutional Partnership** | Revenue-sharing or licensing fees for co-branded content and institutional collaborations |
| 🌍 **Translation Services** | Compensation for verified translations into supported languages (Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic) |
| 🎛️ **Technical & Media Contributions** | Honoraria for audio, video, and multimedia production used in PANORAFUS.AI Studio |
| 💼 **Consulting & Advisory** | Negotiated compensation for theological, organizational, or AI advisory engagements |

## Partnership & Financial Agreements

All formal compensation and financial partnership agreements are:

1. **Documented in writing** with a signed agreement
2. **Reviewed** by the PANORAFUS.AI leadership team
3. **Aligned** with the mission and values of Seasoned Christian Ministry Church
4. **Transparent** — summaries of partnership arrangements may be published in the Global Network Index

To initiate a formal partnership or compensation agreement, contact the primary contact above and include:

- Your name and organization
- A description of the proposed arrangement
- Relevant contact information and availability
