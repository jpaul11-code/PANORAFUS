## Who We Are

**PANORAFUS.AI** is a first-of-its-kind, AI-powered global platform designed to connect, catalogue, and amplify institutions, organizations, and communities worldwide. From churches, mosques, temples, and synagogues to humanitarian organizations, civic bodies, and educational institutions — PANORAFUS.AI is building the world's most comprehensive, living institutional network.

We serve as the digital bridge between:
- **Religious traditions** — Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism, and more
- **Humanitarian organizations** — NGOs, relief networks, and community service bodies
- **Cultural and civic institutions** — Universities, foundations, and global membership organizations

## Business Model

PANORAFUS.AI generates value through a diversified, scalable business model:

### Revenue Streams

| Stream | Model | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Directory Subscriptions** | SaaS / Recurring | Institutions pay to create enhanced, featured profiles in the global network |
| **API Licensing** | B2B / Enterprise | Organizations and developers license access to the PANORAFUS.AI institutional data API |
| **Content Syndication** | Licensing | PANORAFUS network content and data feeds licensed to media, research, and analytics platforms |
| **Advertising & Sponsorship** | CPC / CPM / Direct | Mission-aligned advertising from global faith, civic, and humanitarian organizations |
| **Consulting & Integrations** | Project-Based | Strategic technology partnerships with denominations, NGOs, and global networks |
| **Premium Memberships** | Tiered Subscription | Enhanced visibility, analytics, and outreach tools for premium institutional members |
