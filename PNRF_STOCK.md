## About PANORAFUS.AI (PNRF)

**PANORAFUS.AI** is a global network platform powered by artificial intelligence, designed to connect, catalogue, and amplify institutions, organizations, and communities across the world. It is the digital hub for religious, cultural, educational, and humanitarian networks — bringing the world together under one unified index.

PANORAFUS.AI was founded under the spiritual and organizational leadership of **Seasoned Christian Ministry Church** and is driven by the mission of:

- **Global Connectivity** — Linking religious, cultural, and civic institutions across all nations
- **AI-Powered Discovery** — Using artificial intelligence to make communities worldwide discoverable
- **Institutional Transparency** — Building an open, comprehensive global directory of organizations
- **Faith-Centered Innovation** — Pioneering technology for Kingdom-building and interfaith understanding
