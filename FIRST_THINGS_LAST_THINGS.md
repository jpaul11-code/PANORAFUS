## Vision

**PANORAFUS.AI** presents the beginning and the end under the Lordship of Jesus Christ — the One who is the Alpha and the Omega.

This page gathers the theme **FIRST THINGS - LAST THINGS** as a devotional marker: from creation, calling, and first obedience to judgment, resurrection, and everlasting hope.

## Reflection

- **First Things** remind us to seek God first, honor His Word first, and place Christ first in all understanding.
- **Last Things** remind us that history has an appointed end, that every soul will stand before God, and that the Kingdom of Christ will endure forever.
- **PANORAFUS.AI** holds these truths together so that believers may live with clarity, reverence, and hope.
